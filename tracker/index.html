How to use this (1 minute)
  1. Pick a job → Open posting → apply on the company's site.

  3. Helping someone find a job? Tick Send on good jobs, then tap Send on WhatsApp at the bottom.
  4. Add this page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen).
